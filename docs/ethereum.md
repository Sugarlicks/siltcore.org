# Why This Matters for Ethereum

Ethereum provides programmable execution. Smart contracts, multisigs, DAOs and agentic systems can coordinate actions with considerable technical precision.

But execution does not settle the semantic question of legitimacy.

A wallet can prove control of a key. A multisig can prove that a threshold was met. A token vote can prove that a voting rule executed. None of these facts alone establishes the relational Standing of the Participant, the Source from which that Standing arises, the scope of any Authority, or the conditions under which another party may rely on the action.

SILT Core v0.2 addresses that different layer.

Its settled seam is:

> Source → Standing → Presentation → evaluation at the encounter

A Participant may Present the minimum relevant projection of Standing into an Ethereum-related encounter. One or more Profile Expressions may state the bounded conditions under which Standing, Authority, Consent, Reliance, Action, Attribution, Revocation or Obligation are to be evaluated.

SILT does not replace Ethereum policy, capability or execution systems. Nor does it convert smart contracts into universal legal instruments.

Instead, it preserves semantic continuity at the boundary between plural human, institutional, customary, private and machine systems, while Ethereum performs the technical work it is designed to perform.

**Ethereum can execute a rule. SILT helps make legible what that execution means in the encounter in which it occurs.**
